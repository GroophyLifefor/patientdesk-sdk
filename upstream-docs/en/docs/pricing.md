# Pricing
Alania is billed per character and Duyu per audio hour, drawn from prepaid credit. The first month is free for the launch; prices exclude VAT.

Alania (alania-v2, alania-v1) | 1,000 characters | $0.015 · ₺0,75 | Characters of input; in the x-characters header |

Antalia-2 Mini (antalia-mini) | – | Free | Not charged |
## Prepaid credit
You add credit first, and each request's price comes off it when the request ends. There is no monthly fee, commitment or minimum, and credit does not expire. Credit is held in US dollars.
- Add between $10 and $5,000 on the Billing page. Payment goes through Stripe; VAT is added where it applies, and every top-up gets an invoice.
- Automatic top-up. When the balance drops below the amount you set, credit is added from your saved card; if the card is declined, it pauses and we e-mail you.
- Every new account gets $5 of welcome credit, used once the free first month ends.
- When the credit runs out, Alania and Duyu requests get 402 insufficient_balance; a live stream in progress closes after its next sentence. Antalia-2 Mini stays free.
- We e-mail once when the balance drops under $2. Activity, monthly usage and invoices are on the Billing page.
## Alania
The price is the same in every mode: built-in voice, style instruction, voice design, a cloned or professional voice. Details: Billing.
## Duyu
Files and live streams are priced separately. The billed duration is the length of the audio, not the processing time. Details: Billing.
## Other
Training a professional clone is free during the beta. A dedicated voice is priced per voice and enterprise deployments by quote; for high volume or custom limits, write to sezgin@patientdesk.ai.
