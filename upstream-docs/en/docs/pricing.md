# Pricing
Alania is billed per character and Duyu per audio hour. The first month is free for the launch; prices exclude VAT.
Product | Unit | Price | What counts |
Alania (alania-v2, alania-v1) | 1,000 characters | $0.015 · ₺0,75 | Characters of input; in the x-characters header |
Duyu · files | audio hour | $0.125 · ₺6 | Length of the audio (duration) |
Duyu · live | audio hour | $0.225 · ₺11 | Length of the stream (stopped.audio_s) |
Antalia-2 Mini (antalia-mini) | – | Free | Not counted |
Usage is billed monthly.
## Alania
The price is the same in every mode: built-in voice, style instruction, voice design, a cloned or professional voice. Details: Billing.
## Duyu
Files and live streams are priced separately. The billed duration is the length of the audio, not the processing time. Details: Billing.
## Other
Training a professional clone is free during the beta. A dedicated voice is priced per voice and enterprise deployments by quote; for high volume or custom limits, write to sezgin@patientdesk.ai.
